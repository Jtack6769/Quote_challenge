# 🎬 QUOTE CHALLENGE

### You know the line. Can you name who said it?

That movie you've watched twenty times. That TV character you can hear in your head. That one line you swear you'll never forget.

**Time to put all those hours in front of the screen to work.**

Quote Challenge brings movie and television trivia into a bold, neon-lit arcade atmosphere—with friendly rivalry, familiar voices, and plenty of “I KNOW THIS ONE!” moments.

## ▶️ Step into the challenge

**[PLAY QUOTE CHALLENGE](https://jtack6769.github.io/Quote_challenge/index.html)**

Open the game in your browser, pick your mode, and bring your best movie memory.

## 🍿 Pick your competition

- **One Player:** Take on the computer and see how sharp your memory really is.
- **Two Players · One Phone:** Grab a friend, share the screen, and settle who really knows their movies and TV.
- **Choose your difficulty:** Warm up with the easy ones or test yourself with a tougher challenge.
- **Name the movie or show—and the character:** Recognizing the line is only the beginning.

From old favorites to unforgettable screen moments, this is a game for people who quote along, recognize a voice before a face, and always have time for one more round.
